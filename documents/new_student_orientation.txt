1. WELCOME TO PINNACLE STATE UNIVERSITY

Congratulations on your admission to Pinnacle State University! We are thrilled to welcome you to the Mountaineer family. This orientation guide will help you prepare for your transition to campus life and ensure you have everything you need for a successful start.

Orientation is a required experience for all incoming students. It is designed to help you:
- Meet fellow students and build your first college friendships
- Learn about campus resources and support services
- Register for your first-semester courses with an academic advisor
- Set up your technology accounts and student ID
- Explore campus and discover your new home

Your Orientation Leader (OL) will be your guide throughout the week. They are experienced PSU students who are trained to answer your questions and help you navigate this exciting transition. Don't hesitate to ask them anything — they've been in your shoes!

2. PRE-ARRIVAL CHECKLIST

Complete the following items before arriving on campus. All forms are available through your MyPSU portal at https://my.pinnaclestate.edu.

Before June 15:
□ Accept your admission offer and pay the $200 enrollment deposit
□ Submit your final high school or college transcripts
□ Complete the FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid) at fafsa.gov — PSU code: 001234
□ Review and accept your financial aid package through MyPSU
□ Submit immunization records (MMR, Meningitis, Tdap required — upload to Health Portal)
□ Complete the AlcoholEdu and Sexual Assault Prevention online modules

Before July 15:
□ Apply for on-campus housing through the Housing Portal
□ Select your meal plan (deadline: August 1)
□ Complete the Roommate Preference Questionnaire (if applicable)
□ Upload a photo for your PSU Student ID card (photo specifications on MyPSU)
□ Register for New Student Orientation (select your preferred session)
□ Complete the Math and English Placement Assessments (online, untimed)

Before August 1:
□ Set up your PSU student email account (instructions sent after enrollment deposit)
□ Review your orientation session details and travel/parking information
□ Complete any outstanding health insurance requirements
□ Submit your Emergency Contact Information form through MyPSU
□ Download the PSU Mobile App (available on iOS and Android)
□ Review the Student Handbook and Code of Conduct

You will receive a confirmation email 10 days before your orientation session with:
- Your Orientation Leader's name and contact information
- Detailed daily schedule
- Campus map with parking and check-in locations
- What to bring / what to leave at home
- Weather-appropriate clothing recommendations

3. ORIENTATION WEEK SCHEDULE

Orientation runs for 4 days. Sessions are offered multiple times throughout the summer, with the final session held the week before classes begin.

DAY 1 — WELCOME AND CONNECTIONS (Monday)

8:00 AM — Check-In and Welcome
- Check in at the University Center Grand Ballroom
- Receive your Orientation folder, campus map, and lanyard
- Meet your Orientation Leader and Orientation Group (15–20 students)

9:00 AM — Opening Ceremony
- Welcome address by University President Dr. Robert Whitfield
- Student Government welcome and campus culture introduction
- "What to Expect" panel with current students

10:30 AM — Campus Tour (Walking, ~90 minutes)
- Academic buildings, library, student union
- Dining facilities and recreation center
- Health center and counseling services
- Your assigned residence hall (if living on campus)

12:00 PM — Welcome Lunch (Complimentary)
- Summit Dining Hall — meet students from your Orientation Group
- Dietary accommodations available (indicate during registration)

1:30 PM — PSU 101: University Policies and Resources
- Academic integrity and student conduct expectations
- Title IX overview and campus safety
- Student rights and responsibilities
- How to access help: counseling, tutoring, financial aid

3:00 PM — Diversity and Belonging Workshop
- Interactive session on inclusive community building
- Campus identity centers and cultural organizations
- Allyship and bystander intervention basics

4:30 PM — Free Time and Residence Hall Exploration
- Settle into temporary orientation housing (if staying overnight)
- Explore campus on your own

6:00 PM — Evening Social Event
- BBQ and lawn games on the University Quad
- Live music by PSU student performers
- Ice cream social sponsored by Student Government

DAY 2 — ACADEMICS AND ADVISING (Tuesday)

8:30 AM — Breakfast (Summit Dining Hall)

9:00 AM — Academic Success Workshop
- Time management strategies for college

- Understanding the syllabus and professor expectations

10:30 AM — College/Department Breakout Sessions
- Meet faculty from your intended college/major
- Learn about degree requirements, research opportunities, and student organizations
- Hear from current students in your major

12:00 PM — Lunch with Faculty Mentors
- Small group lunch with professors from your department
- Casual Q&A about academics, careers, and campus life

1:00 PM — Academic Advising and Course Registration
- Individual or small-group advising sessions with professional advisors

3:30 PM — Financial Literacy Workshop

- Browse tables from 50+ student organizations

5. TECHNOLOGY SETUP

5.1 Essential Accounts (Set Up During Orientation)
- PSU Email: firstname.lastname@pinnaclestate.edu (via Microsoft Outlook)
- MyPSU Portal: https://my.pinnaclestate.edu (registration, grades, financial aid)
- Canvas LMS: https://canvas.pinnaclestate.edu (coursework, assignments)
- Microsoft 365: Free suite including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, OneDrive (1 TB storage)
- Zoom: https://pinnaclestate.zoom.us (virtual classes and meetings)

5.2 Device Requirements
PSU recommends the following minimum specifications for personal laptops:
- Operating System: Windows 11 or macOS 13+
- Processor: Intel i5/AMD Ryzen 5 or Apple M1 equivalent or newer
- RAM: 8 GB minimum (16 GB recommended for engineering/design students)
- Storage: 256 GB SSD minimum
- WiFi: 802.11ac (WiFi 5) or newer
- Webcam and microphone (for virtual classes)

The Campus Technology Store (Library, 1st Floor) offers discounted laptops and accessories for PSU students.

5.3 WiFi Setup
Connect to PSU-Secure:
1. Select "PSU-Secure" from available networks
2. Enter your PSU username (Student ID) and password
3. Accept the security certificate when prompted
4. You should be connected within 30 seconds

For gaming consoles, smart TVs, and IoT devices in residence halls, register the device MAC address at https://network.pinnaclestate.edu.

5.4 Printing Setup
1. Go to https://print.pinnaclestate.edu
2. Log in with your PSU credentials
3. Download and install the PaperCut client for your operating system
4. You can now print from your personal device to any campus printer
5. Release your print job at any printer by tapping your PSU ID card

7. FIRST SEMESTER SUCCESS PLAN

WEEKS 1–4: EXPLORE AND ESTABLISH
Goals:
- Attend all classes and review each syllabus carefully
- Visit your professors during office hours (at least once per class)
- Establish a study routine and find your preferred study spaces
- Attend at least 3 student organization meetings to explore interests
- Meet with your academic advisor to confirm your degree plan
- Set up a weekly schedule that balances academics, activities, and self-care

Success Indicators:
- No missed classes in the first month
- Planner or digital calendar in use
- Connected with at least one campus resource
- Comfortable navigating campus independently

WEEKS 5–8: ENGAGE AND GROW
Goals:
- Join at least one student organization and attend regularly
- Use the Academic Success Center for tutoring or writing help if needed
- Prepare for midterm exams — form study groups, attend review sessions
- Attend at least one campus event per week (cultural, social, or athletic)
- Check in with your RA or a trusted mentor about your adjustment

Success Indicators:
- Midterm grades reflect solid effort
- Active membership in at least one organization
- Growing social connections beyond your roommate
- Healthy habits (sleep, exercise, nutrition) established

WEEKS 9–12: DEEPEN AND PLAN
Goals:
- Meet with your advisor to plan your Spring semester courses
- Attend the Spring Registration Workshop
- Explore internship and research opportunities for next semester
- Complete any outstanding financial aid requirements for Spring
- Reflect on your first semester — what's working, what needs adjustment

Success Indicators:
- Spring course registration completed
- Clear plan for academic improvement (if needed)
- Identified at least one leadership or research opportunity
- Financial aid and housing settled for Spring

WEEKS 13–16: FINISH STRONG
Goals:
- Create a final exam study plan (at least 2 weeks before finals)
- Attend all review sessions offered by professors and TAs
- Complete all outstanding assignments and projects
- Plan your winter break (stay connected with PSU friends!)
- Complete the End-of-Semester Course Evaluations (your feedback matters!)

Success Indicators:
- All assignments submitted on time
- Final grades meet your personal goals
- Positive outlook on returning for Spring semester
- At least one meaningful campus connection established

8. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What should I bring to campus?
A: Essentials include bedding (XL Twin for residence halls), toiletries, school supplies, a laptop, a power strip (surge-protected), decorations for your room, and comfortable walking shoes. Leave valuable items, candles, and cooking appliances with open coils at home (check the prohibited items list on the Housing website).

Q: Can I change my major?
A: Yes! Many students change their major, especially during the first two years. Meet with your current advisor and the advisor in your new intended major. Submit a Change of Major form through MyPSU. Most changes take effect the following semester.

Q: How do I get involved on campus?
A: Attend the Campus Involvement Fair during orientation and the first week of classes. Browse the PSU Engage platform (https://engage.pinnaclestate.edu) to see all registered organizations. Most clubs welcome new members at any time during the semester.

Q: What if I'm struggling academically?
A: Reach out early! Visit the Academic Success Center (free tutoring, study skills coaching), talk to your professor during office hours, and meet with your academic advisor. CAPS also offers support for academic stress and anxiety.

Q: How do I get a campus job?
A: Check the Student Employment portal on MyPSU for campus job listings. Federal Work-Study positions are listed separately. The Career Development Center can help with your resume and interview skills. Most campus departments hire student workers — dining, library, recreation center, and ITS are the largest employers.

Q: What if I feel homesick or lonely?
A: This is completely normal! Attend campus events, keep your door open in the residence hall, and give yourself time to adjust. Talk to your RA, Orientation Leader, or a CAPS counselor. Most students find their rhythm within the first 4–6 weeks.

Q: How do I get my textbooks?
A: Check your course syllabi on Canvas for required textbooks. The Campus Bookstore (University Center, 1st Floor) sells new and used copies. You can also rent textbooks, buy digital versions, or check if the library has copies on reserve. Compare prices on sites like Amazon, Chegg, and BookFinder.

Q: Is there a shuttle or transportation service?
A: Yes! The Mountaineer Express shuttle runs 3 routes connecting campus to off-campus housing, downtown Cedar Valley, and the nearest transit hub. The shuttle is free for students (show your PSU ID). Routes run every 15 minutes during peak hours (7:30 AM – 6:00 PM) and every 30 minutes in the evening (until 11:00 PM).

Q: What dining options are available late at night?
A: The Grind coffee shop is open until 11:00 PM daily, and Mountain Market (convenience store) is open until midnight. Vending machines are available in all residence halls 24/7. Several restaurants near campus also deliver.

Q: How do I report a maintenance issue in my residence hall?
A: Submit a work order through the Housing portal on MyPSU or call the Facilities hotline at extension 3200. For emergencies (flooding, no heat, broken locks), call Campus Police at (555) 472-9111.
